Drive Studio

by Juliano Oliveira / DATACG

Drive Studio is a free Google Chrome extension that helps you organize your Google Drive. It adds a panel to Google Drive where you sort your files and folders into categories with unlimited subcategories, and a home page that shows your categories as cards.

The organization is virtual: Drive Studio never moves, renames, or deletes anything in your Drive. Items are stored by their Drive ID, so they stay in place even when a file is moved or renamed.

This page is public and needs no sign-in. Signing in with Google inside the extension is optional.

What Drive Studio is for

Drive Studio's purpose is to help people and small teams find and manage their Google Drive files faster, by grouping them into categories that work like bookmarks, without changing how the files are stored in Drive.

  • Drag files from Drive into categories, and reorder them by dragging.
  • Search with filters by file type, category, and tag.
  • Add tags, notes, status, priority, due date, and assignee to items, and hide items you rarely use.
  • Add links to websites, shown with their favicons.
  • Create Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms directly in a category.
  • Share team pages with colleagues through Google Drive's own sharing.
  • Sync your organization across computers through your own Google account.

How Drive Studio uses Google data

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for sync, team pages, and creating documents. Drive Studio requests only Google's non-sensitive permissions:

  • Email address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected and to identify you as an author on team pages.
  • Drive application data (drive.appdata): to keep a synced copy of your organization in a hidden folder of your own Drive.
  • Files created or opened by Drive Studio (drive.file): to create and update team page files and the documents you create from a category. Drive Studio cannot read the other files in your Drive.
  • Drive "Open with" (drive.install): so team page files open in Drive Studio.

Drive Studio has no server of its own. Requests go directly from your browser to Google, and the developer never receives your data. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Drive Studio has no AI features and does not use Google APIs or Google user data to generate images or any other content. Read the full privacy policy.
