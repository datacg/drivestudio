Drive Studio

by Juliano Oliveira / DATACG

Drive Studio is a Google Chrome extension that adds a panel to Google Drive for organizing your files and folders into categories with unlimited subcategories. It also has a home page with your categories as cards.

The organization is virtual: Drive Studio never moves, renames, or deletes anything in your Drive. Items are stored by their Drive ID, so they stay in place even when a file is moved or renamed.

Features

  • Drag files from Drive into categories, and reorder them by dragging.
  • Search with filters by file type, category, and tag.
  • Add tags and notes, and hide items you rarely use.
  • Add links to websites, shown with their favicons.
  • Sync your organization across computers through your own Google account.

Privacy

Drive Studio has no server of its own. Your organization is stored in your browser and, if you turn on sync, in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only Drive Studio can access. Drive Studio uses only Google's non-sensitive permissions, so it cannot read the other files in your Drive. Read the full privacy policy.

Availability

Drive Studio is free. It is not yet available on the Chrome Web Store.
